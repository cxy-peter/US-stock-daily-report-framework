# Framework Provenance and Fidelity Notes

## 1. Synthesis policy

This framework synthesizes institutional fund-research methods rather than
copying any report's scorecard or historical threshold. Broker research is
used to preserve useful distinctions among legal structure, holdings style,
return-based style, manager responsibility, performance attribution,
portfolio construction and fund-pool governance. Every product fact, legal
rule and threshold must still be verified for the decision date.

The public repository contains methodology only. It does not contain private
filesystem paths, research notes, personal career material, account data or
source inventories.

## 3. Fidelity and conflict rules

- Classification windows and thresholds differ by purpose. Parameterize them
  instead of merging them into one universal label.
- Manager-tenure stitching and co-management weights require evidence about
  actual decision rights.
- A holdings-versus-NAV residual is not automatically trading skill; it may
  contain cash, fees, undisclosed holdings, timing mismatch and model error.
- An unexplained factor residual is not automatically fundamental alpha.
- Current-pool backfills, continuous-tenure samples and future disclosures can
  create survivorship and look-ahead bias.
- Predictive claims require walk-forward or holdout evaluation, disclosure
  lags, costs, capacity and multiple-testing controls.

## 5. Academic constraints

- Fama and French, *Luck versus Skill in the Cross-Section of Mutual Fund
  Returns*: fee-covering alpha is rare in aggregate.
- Carhart, *On Persistence in Mutual Fund Performance*: common factors, fees
  and trading costs explain much apparent persistence.
- Berk and Green, *Mutual Fund Flows and Performance in Rational Markets*:
  skill can coexist with weak investor alpha when scale erodes returns.
- Kacperczyk, Sialm and Zheng, *Unobserved Actions of Mutual Funds*: return gaps
  may contain useful information but are not pure skill.
- Cremers and Petajisto, *How Active Is Your Fund Manager?*: Active Share
  measures benchmark difference, not standalone quality.

## 6. Social-media evidence policy

Reddit, X and other social platforms are discovery sources, not product-fact
authorities. Keep their claims in a separate evidence group, verify them
against primary sources, and never let a social claim independently upgrade,
downgrade or size a fund.
